Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

A Wi-Fi voucher is a digital product that starts counting down as soon as you pay, so we cannot refund time you have already used. We will always put right a payment that did not give you what you paid for.

Keep your order reference. If something goes wrong, the screen shows a reference starting with "ln_". Screenshot it and send it to us.

1. We will refund you if

  • You paid, but no Wi-Fi code was shown or delivered.
  • The voucher length you paid for ran out of stock after payment went through.
  • Your code does not work and we cannot fix it or give you a working one.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.

2. We usually cannot refund

  • A voucher that has been used, fully or partly. Time starts at payment and cannot be paused.
  • Time lost because you did not use the code, lost the code, or did not screenshot it. The code is shown once.
  • Slow speeds or short outages caused by the internet provider or the power grid. If a long outage cuts into your paid time, tell us and we will review it fairly, and may extend your time or refund the unused part.
  • Vouchers cancelled because of misuse, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

3. Subscriptions

Weekly, monthly and yearly plans are arranged with us directly. If you stop using a plan early, we will look at a refund for the unused full weeks or months, depending on what you agreed with us. Please contact us before the plan starts if you want the refund terms confirmed in writing.

4. How to ask for a refund

  • Message us on WhatsApp or email us within 7 days of the payment. See the Contact page.
  • Send your order reference (starts with "ln_"), the voucher length, the amount, and the time you paid. A screenshot of the Paystack receipt or your bank alert also works.
  • We will reply within 2 working days.

5. How you get the money back

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack. It usually takes 5 to 10 working days to reach your account, depending on your bank. Where it is faster for you and we agree, we may instead send you a replacement code.

6. If we say no

We will tell you why. If you still disagree, tell us and the owner will review it personally.
