Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

We collect as little as we can. You can buy a voucher on our website without giving us your name, email or phone number.

1. Who is responsible

Las Noches Limited, Minna, Niger State, Nigeria, is responsible for your information. We handle it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What we collect

  • When you buy a voucher online: an order reference, the voucher length, the amount paid, the payment status, the time of purchase, and the Wi-Fi code issued to that order. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Payment details: you enter these on Paystack's secure page. We never see or store your card number, bank details or PIN. Paystack gives us only the result of the payment.
  • When you visit the space: our front desk may record your visit, such as the seat you used and the time and voucher code, so we can manage seating and Wi-Fi access.
  • When you message us: if you contact us on WhatsApp or email, we see the details you send, such as your number and your message.
  • Subscriptions: if you take a weekly, monthly or yearly plan, we may ask for your name and phone number so we can manage your plan.

3. How we use it

  • To process your payment and give you the Wi-Fi code you paid for.
  • To manage seats and Wi-Fi access in the space.
  • To answer your questions and sort out problems or refunds.
  • To keep records we need for accounting and the law.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse of our vouchers and network.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Paystack processes payments for us. See Paystack's privacy policy.
  • Supabase hosts our database, where order and entry records are stored.
  • Our hosting provider serves this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site, so your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Cookies and tracking

Our website does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not set tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for as long as we need them for accounting, disputes and legal requirements. We keep other records only as long as they are useful for running the space.

7. Keeping it safe

Our database is access-controlled, staff sign in with protected accounts, and payments run through Paystack. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

8. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Because online voucher orders are not linked to your name or contact details, we may need your order reference to find your records. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

Our website is not aimed at children. Anyone under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission to buy a voucher.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, use our Contact page.
